AC Repair

What It Is

Fast Fixes That Actually Last

AC repair is diagnosing and fixing any fault in your existing air conditioning system. From a unit that won't turn on to one that blows warm air — we find the root cause and fix it right the first time.

We work on all major brands and models. Our technicians carry the most common parts on their trucks so most repairs are completed in a single visit.

Common Problems We Fix

If you're experiencing any of these, call us today.

Warm Air Blowing

Usually a refrigerant leak or compressor fault. We locate the issue and restore your cool air fast.

Water Leaks

Clogged drain lines or frozen evaporator coils cause leaks. Left unchecked, they damage walls and floors.

Strange Noises

Banging, rattling, or squealing usually means a loose or failing component. We fix it before it becomes expensive.

Heating Repair

What It Is

Stay Warm When It Matters Most

Heating repair covers diagnosis and fixing of furnaces, heat pumps, and any heating component in your HVAC system. When Houston temperatures drop, a broken heater isn't just uncomfortable — it can be dangerous for families and elderly residents.

We respond fast, diagnose accurately, and repair correctly so you're not left in the cold waiting days for a fix.

Signs Your Heat Needs Help

Don't wait until it completely stops — these are early warning signs.

No Heat Coming Out

System runs but produces no warmth — usually a failed heating element, ignitor, or gas valve issue.

Bills Suddenly Spike

A struggling heater works overtime to reach temperature — costing you money every hour it runs inefficiently.

Short Cycling

System turns on and off rapidly without reaching target temperature — a sign of overheating or sensor failure.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking a service.

How quickly can you come out for a repair?
For standard repairs we typically arrive same day or next day. For emergencies we aim to be on-site within 2 hours, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week including public holidays.
Do you provide a quote before starting work?
Absolutely. We never start any work without your approval. After diagnosing the issue we provide a full written quote. The price we quote is the price you pay — no hidden fees.
How long does a full AC installation take?
Most residential AC installations are completed in a single day — typically 4 to 8 hours depending on the size of the system and any additional ductwork required.
What warranty do you offer on your work?
All labour comes with a 1-year warranty. Equipment and parts are covered by the manufacturer's warranty — typically 5 to 10 years depending on the brand.
What areas of Houston do you cover?
We cover the entire Greater Houston area including Katy, Sugar Land, Pearland, The Woodlands, Pasadena, Friendswood, and all surrounding neighbourhoods.
Do you service all AC brands and models?
Yes. Our technicians work on all major brands including Carrier, Trane, Lennox, Goodman, Rheem, York, and more. We carry the most common parts on our trucks for faster repairs.
How much does a maintenance plan cost?
Our maintenance plans start at $99/year for two seasonal visits including full inspection, cleaning, and a performance report. Plan members get priority scheduling and 10% off repairs.
Is there a charge for a diagnostic visit?
We charge a small diagnostic fee which is waived if you proceed with the repair. Installation assessments and quotes are completely free with no obligation.
